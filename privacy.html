Privacy policy

Kakitori, an iPad app. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Kakitori doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app has no server.

What stays where

  • On your iPad: your words, mnemonics, reviews, schedules, handwriting and settings.
  • In your own iCloud, if iCloud sync is on (it is by default, and can be turned off in Settings): your words, schedules and reviews, so they're the same on all your iPads. This is stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can read. The developer can't see it. Handwriting isn't synced.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. The app only learns what you've bought, never who you are or how you paid.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything on the iPad. To remove the iCloud copy: the iPad's Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Kakitori.

Children

Kakitori collects nothing from anyone, including children.
