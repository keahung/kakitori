Help

The tutorial runs on first launch, and again any time from Settings › Show the tutorial.

Purchases

Kakitori is free to download with a 14-day free trial of practice. After that, practice needs a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription. Adding words, Progress, backups and iCloud sync stay free.

To restore a purchase on a new iPad: Settings › Restore purchases. To cancel the yearly subscription: the iPad's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
